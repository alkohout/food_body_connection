Allergen Importance

The allergens you have been exposed to are ranked (Figure ), showing which allergens are most likely to trigger symptoms within 24 hours of exposure. The bars represent the strength of association, called the "odds ratio," which indicates how much more likely symptoms are when the allergen is consumed. An odds ratio greater than 1 suggests the allergen may increase symptom risk, while an odds ratio around 1 (dashed line) indicates little or no effect. Use this information to explore potential triggers, but remember this shows association, not a diagnosis.

Symptom frequency

This chart summarises how often symptoms are recorded after exposure to different allergens. For each allergen, we count symptoms that occur within 24 hours of an exposure and normalise by how often that allergen was consumed, allowing fair comparison across foods.
